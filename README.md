# Pakistan Gold & Silver Rates (Sarafa)

Open data on the 24K gold rate and silver rate per tola in Pakistan, as announced by the All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association (APGJSA). Maintained by [SarafaRate](https://sarafarate.com/).

- **Live daily rate, karat and city tables:** https://sarafarate.com/
- **Full history with charts:** https://sarafarate.com/gold-rate-history/
- **Citable archive (Zenodo):** [10.5281/zenodo.24461459](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24461459)

## Files

| File | What it contains |
|---|---|
| [`data/pakistan_gold_rate_year_end_2019_2025.csv`](data/pakistan_gold_rate_year_end_2019_2025.csv) | 24K gold rate per tola on the last trading day of each year, 2019–2025. Each figure is backed by two independent news reports, with links. Where they differ (2022), both are given. |
| [`data/pakistan_sarafa_gold_silver_daily.csv`](data/pakistan_sarafa_gold_silver_daily.csv) | Daily rates from 25 September 2026: 24K gold per tola, silver per tola and (where recorded) the international gold price converted to rupees per tola when the local rate was confirmed. Only days with an announced rate are listed. |

24K gold rose about 5.2 times from the end of 2019 to the end of 2025.

## Method

- 1 tola = 11.664 grams. All prices are in Pakistani rupees.
- These are association rates, not jeweller retail prices (which add making charges).
- Each daily rate was confirmed by two independent sources before it was published on SarafaRate. Corrections are logged publicly at https://sarafarate.com/our-record/.
- Other karats: 22K ≈ 24K × 22/24, 21K ≈ 24K × 21/24, 18K ≈ 24K × 18/24.
